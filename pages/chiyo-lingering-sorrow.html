📝 Historical Notes

Panel 1: Warm broths (温かい汁物 atatakai shirumono) were a cornerstone of Edo-period folk medicine. Miso-based soups with medicinal herbs were prescribed for everything from grief-related ailments to seasonal depression.
Panel 3: The bamboo-versus-oak metaphor (竹と樫 take to kashi) is a recurring theme in Japanese philosophy. Bamboo's flexibility under pressure symbolizes resilience through adaptability rather than rigid strength—a core principle in both bushido and Buddhist thought.
