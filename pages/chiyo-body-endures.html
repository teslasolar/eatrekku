📝 Historical Notes

Panel 1: Willow bark (柳皮 ryūhi) contains salicin, a natural precursor to aspirin. It was used across Edo-period Japan for pain relief, fever reduction, and inflammation—centuries before Western medicine isolated the compound in 1828.
Panel 4: Rehabilitation through daily physical activity mirrors Edo-era practices where herbalists combined medicine with movement therapy. Patients were often encouraged to walk, garden, or perform simple crafts as part of their recovery.
