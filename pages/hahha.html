📝 Historical Notes

Panel 1: Laughter (笑い warai) was considered medicinal in Edo Japan. The rakugo (落語) tradition—comic storytelling—flourished during the Genroku era, with performers like Tsuyu no Gorobei drawing massive crowds to yose theaters. The act of laughing together was seen as a communal healing practice.
